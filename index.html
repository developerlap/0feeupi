Free UPI QR Code Generator with Automatic Amount Splitting

This free UPI QR code generator lets shopkeepers, freelancers and small businesses collect UPI payments of any size. Enter your UPI ID, account holder name and the full amount — the tool instantly creates ready-to-scan QR codes with the amount already filled in, so customers only have to scan and confirm.

Why split a big payment into multiple UPI QR codes?

Many UPI apps and bank accounts enforce per-transaction limits, and big single transactions often fail or get flagged. This tool automatically splits your total into smaller QR codes so that no single QR code exceeds your chosen limit (default ₹1,099) — while the combined total remains exactly what you are owed. For example, ₹4,500 becomes four QRs of ₹1,099 and one QR of ₹104.

  • ✅ Works with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and every UPI app
  • ✅ Amount pre-filled inside each QR — no typing mistakes by customers
  • ✅ Download each QR as PNG or print them all on one sheet
  • ✅ 100% browser-based — your UPI ID is never uploaded anywhere
